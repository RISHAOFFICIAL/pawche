## Visual Requirements for Designer
1.  **Uniformity:** Every image must use the exact hex codes #000000 and #C9A84C.
2.  **Typography:** Use serif fonts for headlines (Aspirational/Classic) and clean sans-serif for sub-details.
3.  **Lighting:** Prioritize "Rembrandt" or "Low-key" lighting for mood. Gold should always have a 'radiant' glow or sheen.
4.  **Models:** Focus on breeds that fit the aesthetic (Frenchies, Greyhounds, Dobermans, Poodles).
