# PAWCHÉ SOCIAL MEDIA VISUAL STYLE GUIDE

This guide defines the visual standards for all Pawché social media channels (Instagram, TikTok, Facebook). Adhering to these standards ensures a consistent ultra-luxury aesthetic that resonates with our high-net-worth target audience.

---

## 1. CORE VISUAL IDENTITY

### **A. Color Palette**
*   **Obsidian Black (#000000):** The primary background color. Represents mystery, power, and elegance.
*   **Radiant Gold (#C9A84C):** The primary accent color. Used for typography, borders, and highlights.
*   **Deep Charcoal (#1A1A1A):** Secondary background/texture color for depth.

### **B. Typography**
*   **Headings:** High-contrast Serif (e.g., Cinzel, Bodoni, Playfair Display).
    *   *Usage:* Headlines, Collection Names, "The Club" mentions.
*   **Body / Sub-headings:** Clean Geometric Sans-Serif (e.g., Montserrat, Futura, Helvetica).
    *   *Usage:* Product details, CTAs, long-form copy.

### **C. Imagery Style**
*   **Editorial Standard:** High-fidelity, studio-quality photography.
*   **Lighting:** Cinematic lighting with dramatic shadows and soft highlights on textures (velvet, gold, leather).
*   **Composition:** Minimalist backgrounds (pure black, deep charcoal, or luxury interiors).
*   **Models:** Well-groomed dogs in poised, regal stances.

---

## 2. INSTAGRAM GRID STRATEGY (3x3 PATTERN)

To maintain a luxury "Atelier" feel, use the following rotating pattern:

1.  **Macro Detail:** Close-up of a product (e.g., the gold clasp of a collar, the texture of a velvet bed).
2.  **Dog Model:** Full model shot in a high-end setting.
3.  **Typography / Quote:** A minimal black square with gold serif text (e.g., "Your Dog Lives for Luxury Too.").
4.  **Product Hero:** Studio-lit product shot on a pure black background.
5.  **Atmospheric / Teaser:** Non-product shot that sets the mood (e.g., silk drapes, architectural detail).
6.  **Dog Model:** Close-up of a dog wearing an accessory.
7.  **Typography / Brand Mission:** Statement about quality or craftsmanship.
8.  **Macro Detail:** Another texture-focused shot.
9.  **Product Collection:** Multiple items from one collection (The Wardrobe, The Vault, etc.).

---

## 3. TIKTOK & REEL OVERLAY STYLE

*   **Font:** Use the Serif font for titles and Sans-Serif for captions.
*   **Text Color:** Radiant Gold (#C9A84C) or White on Black.
*   **Transitions:**
    *   *Standard:* Smooth cross-dissolves (0.5s - 1s).
    *   *Luxury Slide:* Slow, cinematic pans or zoom-ins.
    *   *Beat Match:* Cut only on high-quality ambient or orchestral luxury soundtracks.
*   **Color Grading:** Low saturation, high contrast, warm gold highlights.

---

## 4. TEMPLATE DEFINITIONS

### **Instagram Stories**
*   **Product Drops:** High-contrast image with centered Gold Serif text: "NEW ACQUISITION: [Product Name]".
*   **Inner Circle Highlights:** Featured customer photo in a gold-bordered frame with "PATRON SPOTLIGHT".
*   **Interactive:** Gold-toned polls or Q&A boxes on black backgrounds.

### **Video Overlays**
*   **Corner Logo:** Small gold Pawché logo in the top-right corner.
*   **Price Reveal:** Fade-in gold text at the bottom-center.

---

## 5. REVENUE-DRIVING CTAS

Always use elevated language for CTAs:
*   Instead of "Buy Now" → use **"Acquire Now"** or **"Reserve Your Piece"**.
*   Instead of "Join our newsletter" → use **"Enter The Inner Circle"**.
*   Instead of "Shop the sale" → use **"Curated Reductions"**.
