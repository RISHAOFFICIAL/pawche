# Pawché Social Commerce & Tracking Launch Guide (Phase 8)

This guide provides step-by-step instructions for the store owner to finalize the connection between **Pawché** and your social commerce channels (Facebook, Instagram, TikTok) and set up high-accuracy tracking pixels.

---

## 1. Meta (Facebook & Instagram) Shopping Setup
Meta integration allows you to sell directly on Facebook and Instagram and enables product tagging in your posts and stories.

### **Step 1: Install & Connect**
1. In your Shopify Admin, go to **Sales Channels** and search for **Facebook & Instagram**.
2. Click **Add Channel**.
3. Click **Connect Account** and log in with your Meta Business account credentials.
4. Select the **Pawché Facebook Page** and your **Instagram Professional Account**.

### **Step 2: Enable Commerce Features**
1. Within the Facebook & Instagram app settings, navigate to **Feature Setup**.
2. Enable **Instagram Shopping** and **Facebook Shop**.
3. Meta will review your account (usually takes 24-48 hours). Once approved, your products will appear in a "Shop" tab on your profiles.

### **Step 3: Meta Pixel & Conversions API (CAPI)**
1. Go to **Settings > Data Sharing** in the Facebook & Instagram app.
2. Select **Maximum** for the data-sharing level. This enables the Conversions API for more accurate tracking, bypassing some browser limitations.
3. Select your **Meta Pixel**. If you don't have one, follow the prompts to create it.
4. Verify that the Pixel ID matches your Meta Events Manager.

---

## 2. TikTok Shop & Marketing Setup
TikTok integration is crucial for reaching the urban professional demographic.

### **Step 1: Install TikTok Channel**
1. In Shopify Admin, add the **TikTok** sales channel.
2. Click **Connect TikTok For Business** and log in.
3. Connect your **TikTok Business Center** and **Ad Account**.

### **Step 2: Activate TikTok Shop**
1. In the TikTok channel settings, navigate to **TikTok Shop**.
2. Complete the setup requirements (Business registration info may be required).
3. Once approved, your products from 'The Wardrobe' and 'The Vault' will sync automatically to your TikTok Shop.

### **Step 3: TikTok Pixel**
1. In the TikTok channel settings, navigate to **Settings > Data Sharing**.
2. Create or select your **TikTok Pixel**.
3. Ensure **Advanced Matching** is turned ON to improve ad attribution and retargeting performance.

---

## 3. Catalog Sync & Product Tagging
We have tagged all **87 items** in the master catalog as `Social Commerce` to ensure they are prioritized for sync.

### **Catalog Synchronization**
- Your products will automatically sync to Meta Commerce Manager and TikTok Shop.
- **Pro Tip**: Use "Product Sets" in Meta Commerce Manager to group items like "The Wardrobe" or "New Arrivals" for specific ad campaigns.

### **Instagram Product Tagging**
- Once approved for Instagram Shopping, you can tag products directly in your posts:
    1. Upload a photo/video.
    2. Tap **Tag Products**.
    3. Tap the product in the photo.
    4. Search for the product name (e.g., "The Heirloom Pearl Gala Collar") and select it.
    5. Share!

---

## 4. Final Verification
1. Visit your Meta Commerce Manager and TikTok Seller Center to ensure all **87 products** are imported without errors.
2. Use the **Meta Pixel Helper** and **TikTok Pixel Helper** browser extensions to verify that tracking is active on `pawche.store`.

**Note**: All technical groundwork (CSV tagging, collection mapping) is complete. The connections above require your manual login for security and authorization.
