# Pawché Technical Launch Guide: Phase 4 & 5

This guide provides the final configuration steps for **Legal Policies**, **Payments**, and **Shipping**. These settings must be finalized in the Shopify Admin dashboard to ensure a smooth launch.

---

## Phase 4: Legal & Store Policies

Shopify requires these policies to be officially "published" in the Legal settings to activate them across the checkout and footer.

### **Steps to Publish:**
1. Go to **Settings > Legal** in your Shopify Admin.
2. For each policy below, copy the text from the corresponding section and paste it into the text box.
3. Click **Save** at the top right of the page.

## Phase 5: Payments & Shipping Configuration

### **1. Shipping Setup**
1. Go to **Settings > Shipping and Delivery**.
2. **Shipping Origin:** Click **Change location** and set the address to your Detroit, Michigan HQ.
3. **Shipping Zones (Domestic):** Create or edit a zone for the United States.
4. **Add Rates:**
    *   **Rate 1:** Name: "Standard Shipping", Price: **$6.99**, Condition: Based on order price ($0.00 - $74.99).
    *   **Rate 2:** Name: "Complimentary Shipping", Price: **$0.00**, Condition: Based on order price ($75.00 and up).
5. Click **Save**.

### **2. Payment Setup**
1. Go to **Settings > Payments**.
2. **Shopify Payments:** Click **Activate Shopify Payments**. Ensure Visa, Mastercard, AMEX, Apple Pay, and Google Pay are enabled.
3. **Shop Pay:** Enable **Shop Pay** for accelerated checkout.
4. **PayPal:** Click **Activate PayPal Express Checkout**.
5. **BNPL (Buy Now, Pay Later):** We recommend activating **Klarna** or **Afterpay** via the Shopify Payments settings to help customers purchase high-ticket items (from The Vault or The Penthouse Suite).

### **3. Taxes**
1. Go to **Settings > Taxes and duties**.
2. Ensure **United States** is selected. Shopify will automatically calculate sales tax for Michigan (and other states if you reach nexus).
