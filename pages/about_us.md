# About Pawché

Welcome to Pawché, where the canine lifestyle meets high-fashion. We believe that true luxury isn't just for you—it’s for your most loyal companion too. Inspired by the sophistication of the world's most iconic fashion houses, Pawché was born from a simple realization: **Your Dog Lives for Luxury Too.**

## The Vision
Our curated collection features only the most exquisite pieces, from designer couture and gold-toned jewelry to velvet loungers and marble feeding stations. We don't just sell pet products; we provide a lifestyle upgrade for the dog that deserves everything.

## Our Standards
Every item in The Vault, The Wardrobe, and beyond is selected for its superior craftsmanship and timeless aesthetic. With a palette of deep obsidian and radiant gold, Pawché is more than a brand—it's a statement.

Welcome to the club. Your dog has been waiting.
