# Pawché Size Guide

At Pawché, we understand that a perfect fit is the ultimate luxury. Use our guide below to ensure your companion wears their couture with confidence.

## Measurement Tips
- **Neck**: Measure around the thickest part of the neck where the collar sits.
- **Chest**: Measure around the widest part of the ribcage, just behind the front legs.
- **Length**: Measure from the base of the neck to the base of the tail.

## Standard Size Chart

| Size | Weight (lbs) | Chest (in) | Neck (in) | Length (in) | Breed Example |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **XS** | 3 - 6 | 10" - 12" | 7" - 9" | 7" - 9" | Chihuahua, Teacup Yorkie |
| **S** | 7 - 12 | 13" - 15" | 10" - 12" | 10" - 12" | Maltese, Pomeranian, Toy Poodle |
| **M** | 13 - 20 | 16" - 18" | 13" - 15" | 13" - 15" | French Bulldog, Pug, Boston Terrier |
| **L** | 21 - 35 | 19" - 21" | 16" - 18" | 16" - 18" | Beagle, Cocker Spaniel, Shiba Inu |
| **XL** | 36 - 55 | 22" - 25" | 19" - 21" | 19" - 21" | Bulldog, Border Collie, Springer Spaniel |
| **XXL** | 55+ | 26"+ | 22"+ | 22"+ | Golden Retriever, Labrador, German Shepherd |

*Note: If your dog is between sizes, we recommend choosing the larger size for a more comfortable, editorial drape.*

## Accessory Sizing
- **Collars & Jewelry**: Please ensure two fingers can fit comfortably between the collar and your dog's neck.
- **Beds**: Choose a size that allows your dog to stretch out fully. When in doubt, upgrade to the Penthouse Suite (XXL).
