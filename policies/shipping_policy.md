# Shipping Policy

Welcome to the world of Pawché luxury. We are committed to delivering our exquisite collections to your doorstep with care and efficiency.

## Shipping Rates & Delivery

We offer straightforward shipping options to ensure your dog's luxury essentials arrive promptly.

*   **Standard Shipping (Orders under $75):** Flat rate of **$6.99**.
*   **Complimentary Shipping:** We are pleased to offer **Free Shipping** on all orders over **$75**.

## Shipping Origin
All Pawché orders are processed and dispatched from our logistics center in **Detroit, Michigan**.

## Delivery Times
Most orders are processed within 1-3 business days. Delivery typically takes 5-10 business days, depending on your location. You will receive a tracking number via email once your order has shipped so you can monitor its journey.

## International Shipping
Currently, we primarily serve customers within the United States. For international inquiries, please contact our concierge team at support@pawche.store.

## Order Tracking
Once your order is on its way, you will receive a shipping confirmation email with a link to track your package in real-time.

## Missing or Delayed Packages
If your package is delayed or goes missing, please contact us at support@pawche.store, and we will work with the carrier to resolve the issue as quickly as possible.

Your dog's luxury lifestyle is our priority. Thank you for shopping with Pawché.
