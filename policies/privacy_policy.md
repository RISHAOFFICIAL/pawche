# Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy describes how Pawché (the "Site", "we", "us", or "our") collects, uses, and discloses your personal information when you visit or make a purchase from pawche.store.

## 1. Information We Collect
When you visit the Site, we collect certain information about your device, your interaction with the Site, and information necessary to process your purchases.

*   **Device Information:** Examples include version of web browser, IP address, time zone, cookie information, what sites or products you view, search terms, and how you interact with the Site.
*   **Order Information:** Examples include name, billing address, shipping address, payment information (including credit card numbers, Shop Pay, PayPal), email address, and phone number.
*   **Customer Support Information:** Any information you provide to our concierge team during interactions.

## 2. How We Use Your Information
We use your personal information to provide our luxury services to you, which includes: offering products for sale, processing payments, shipping and fulfillment of your order, and keeping you up to date on new products, services, and offers from "The Club."

## 3. Sharing Personal Information
We share your Personal Information with service providers to help us provide our services and fulfill our contracts with you, as described above. For example:
*   We use Shopify to power our online store.
*   We may share your Personal Information to comply with applicable laws and regulations.

## 4. Behavioral Advertising
As described above, we use your Personal Information to provide you with targeted advertisements or marketing communications we believe may be of interest to you.

## 5. Your Rights
If you are a resident of certain jurisdictions, you have the right to access the Personal Information we hold about you, to port it to a new service, and to ask that your Personal Information be corrected, updated, or erased.

## 6. Cookies
A cookie is a small amount of information that’s downloaded to your computer or device when you visit our Site. We use a number of different cookies, including functional, performance, advertising, and social media or content cookies.

## 7. Changes
We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time in order to reflect, for example, changes to our practices or for other operational, legal, or regulatory reasons.

## 8. Contact
For more information about our privacy practices, if you have questions, or if you would like to make a complaint, please contact us by e-mail at privacy@pawche.store.
