# Refund and Return Policy

At Pawché, we believe that your dog deserves the best, and that includes a seamless and luxurious shopping experience. We stand behind the quality of our curated collections and want you to be completely satisfied with your purchase.

## Luxury-Friendly Returns
If you or your canine companion are not entirely satisfied with your order, we offer a "no-hassle" return process. We accept returns of unworn, unwashed, and undamaged items with original tags attached within **30 days** of delivery.

## Easy Return Process
1. **Initiate Your Return:** Contact our concierge team at support@pawche.store with your order number and the items you wish to return.
2. **Receive Your Label:** We will provide you with a pre-paid return shipping label for your convenience.
3. **Ship It Back:** Securely package your items and drop them off at the designated carrier.

## Refunds
Once we receive and inspect your return, we will process your refund immediately. A credit will automatically be applied to your original method of payment within 5-7 business days.

## Exchanges
Need a different size for The Wardrobe? We offer free exchanges to ensure the perfect fit for your pet. Contact us, and we'll handle the rest.

## Non-Returnable Items
For hygiene reasons, certain items such as opened grooming products from The Spa or used bedding from The Sleep Suite cannot be returned unless they arrive damaged or defective.

## Damaged or Defective Items
In the rare event that an item arrives in less than perfect condition, please contact us immediately so we can make it right.

Thank you for choosing Pawché. Your dog lives for luxury too.
